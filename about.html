Our story

Made for lovers who feel everything

Lovesick began with a simple belief: the best wedding images aren't the perfect ones — they're the true ones.

We work quietly and intentionally, guiding when you need it and stepping back when the moment is happening on its own. Our style is elegant, editorial and warm — light-filled frames that still feel like you in twenty years.

Photography and film under one name means one calm, cohesive team on your day, and one story told beautifully from every angle.
